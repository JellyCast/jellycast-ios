# Contributing

Ideas and improvements are welcome. Open an issue or a pull request. If it fits the order of priority, **works well > responds instantly > looks good**, I'm happy to take a look.

## Before your first pull request

JellyCast for iOS is sold in the App Store, and its source is available under the [PolyForm Shield License 1.0.0](LICENSE). So that your work can ship in the App Store version, every contributor agrees to the [Contributor License Agreement](CLA.md) once. You keep the copyright in your work; the agreement lets the maintainer include it in the app and move the project to a more open license later.

Put this line in the description of your first pull request:

> I have read and agree to the Contributor License Agreement in CLA.md.

## Working on the code

- [CLAUDE.md](CLAUDE.md) has the stack, the layout and the commands.
- [.claude/rules/](.claude/rules/) has the product principles, style and code rules.
- [NOTES.md](NOTES.md) has the decisions so far and why they were made.

Keep a pull request to one change, and say in its description what you tested and on which device.

## Forking

Forking for your own use needs no agreement and no pull request: build the app and put it on your own devices. Offering a fork to others, in the App Store or anywhere else, is not allowed; see [LICENSE](LICENSE).
